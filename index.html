Support Performance Dashboard: cheat sheet

Your goals (KPIs) are measured every quarter. Formal measurement starts after your first 90 days.

NumberWhat it meansGoalHow to meet it
UtilizationHours logged in My Day ÷ available hours. Available = 8 h × business days, minus PTO, through yesterday.90–100%Log training and planned time at the start of your shift, log every case as you work it, and check your total before you log off.
Cases touchedDistinct cases you posted on (note, link or file) or emailed from. Each case counts once.—Clean your queue every day: Express and Critical first, then Standard.
Monthly paceCases touched ÷ available days × 21.~200–250 / month at full workloadPost an update on every case you move forward. Pace grows as you learn.
TTS StandardPortal cases acknowledged within 1 business day (24 h, no weekends or US holidays).90–100%List View first thing, sorted by Due Date. Milestone plus a customer-visible note.
TTS Express / Critical1 hour / 30 minutes. Nonstop for 24/7 customers, otherwise Mon–Fri 7 a.m.–7 p.m. ET.100%Always first. Acknowledge right away, then work the case.
Billable h / % BillableHours logged as Billable ÷ logged hours.No targetJust use the Billable category correctly.
New / ClosedCases with your My Day time that came in, or closed, in the period.—Follow up on overdue cases and close what you validated.

Support also helps the company meet its quarterly OKRs. They’re explained to you once they’re made and approved.

Questions about your numbers? Ask: Support team, Tier II or Director of Customer Support.
